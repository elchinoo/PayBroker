## 4 am_eric
Every one of those wrote a ledger entry. Decisions, captures, refunds. Also rule changes, and key creation.

Each entry is written in the same database transaction as the change itself. So a decision cannot exist without its entry.

And each entry carries the SHA-256 hash of the entry before it. That is a chain.

Now I press Verify.

[pause 2.0]

The database recomputes every hash. Chain intact.

Here is the same check in plain SQL.

[pause 2.5]

A function, a trigger, and a hash. There is no external ledger service to run.

## 7 am_adam
Next problem. Acme Shop must never see another merchant's payments. Even if a developer forgets the filter.

Row-level security does that, inside the database. There are four rules. A merchant sees only its own rows. Child rows follow their payment. Cases and chargebacks belong to the operator. And read-only auditors may look, never change.

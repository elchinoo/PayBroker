## 1 am_eric
I run a marketplace. Card fraud and false declines cost me money. And I will not ship payment data to three vendors.

...

So here is how Postgres on AgentPG does it. That is the platform part, with its EDB extensions. PayBroker is the sample app on top.

Three things here are scaffolding you can swap: sign-in, the model gateway, and the secret vault.

Sign-in speaks standard OpenID Connect. Any provider works, as long as it sends the roles our apps expect. The gateway speaks an OpenAI-compatible API. Both are set by configuration.

The vault is a small module of the shared kit. It is replaceable, but not by a setting.

It uses only test cards, so nothing here moves real money.

## 9 af_sarah
Now, under the hood. Here is a problem the analyst has. Have we seen payments like this one before?

Postgres answers it, with pgvector. Every payment is stored as a list of seventeen numbers.

This index finds the nearest neighbours fast. It is HNSW, with cosine distance.

[pause 1.5]

And here is the real query from the app. It sorts past payments by distance, and keeps the five nearest flagged ones.

[pause 2.0]

Each row shows how close it is, and its risk category.

Those five go to the triage. No second system, and no copy of the data.
